04 — SELECTED WORK

CAPSTONE PROJECT

PRODUCT STRATEGY · UX CONCEPT

Rapido ride growth

BENGALURU, INDIA
THE BRIEF

Grow rides while making the experience work better for passengers and captains.

Capstone project exploring how Rapido could grow its Bengaluru bike taxi business through marketplace improvements and clearer app experiences.

500K → 1Mmonthly rides target
within 12 months
CHALLENGE

Research highlighted ride availability and wait times, captain cancellations, weather disruption, safety concerns, payment friction, support delays, and map accuracy as key experience issues.

ACTIONS

Synthesized market and user research, personas, and survey insights; prioritized growth opportunities; and outlined passenger and captain product concepts.

Proposed faster booking, fare estimates, live tracking, flexible payment, trip sharing, captain onboarding, training, incentive tracking, and earnings visibility.

DELIVERABLES

Research synthesis, prioritized recommendations, user flows, wireframe framework, app mockup concepts, and launch measurement ideas.

SKILLS
Product strategyUser researchPrioritizationUX flowsWireframingGrowth metrics

Concept work only: the recommendations and mockups were not presented as a live launch. Ride growth and other success measures are proposed targets, not achieved results.
