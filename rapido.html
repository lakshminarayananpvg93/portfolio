THE PRODUCT CHALLENGE

Grow demand without losing sight of the marketplace.

The brief set a goal to increase completed bike taxi rides in Bengaluru from 500,000 to 1,000,000 per month within 12 months. Meeting that ambition would require a dependable experience for both riders and captains, especially through busy periods and changing travel conditions.

MY CONTRIBUTION

From research to a prioritized product concept.

This capstone brought together market and user research, survey findings, personas, a growth plan, and passenger and captain app concepts. I translated the findings into experience opportunities and proposed measures for a potential launch.

CHALLENGES

Where the experience could break down.

01

Ride availability

High-demand areas and rush hours could mean longer waits and difficulty finding a ride.

02

Cancellations

Captain cancellations—including declines on longer trips—could add friction and uncertainty.

03

Trust & safety

Safety concerns, captain behavior, payment issues, and slow support affected confidence in the service.

04

Weather & location

Rain could disrupt bike trips, while inaccurate maps and pickup locations could increase wait time.

PRODUCT DECISIONS

Design both sides of the ride.

For passengers

  • Fast booking with fare estimates and flexible payment options.
  • Live ride tracking, pickup ETA, rider details, and trip sharing.
  • Ride history, feedback, customer support, and emergency access.

For captains

  • Simpler onboarding with training and document verification.
  • A dashboard for performance, incentives, and goals.
  • Clear earnings and payout tracking, plus access to support and training.

The capstone also proposed reducing captain commissions, improving map accuracy, addressing long pickup distances, and investing in captain support.

OUTCOMES & MEASUREMENT

A concept with a measurable north star.

The project delivered research synthesis, prioritized recommendations, user flow and wireframe concepts, and a launch measurement plan. The brief’s north-star target was 1,000,000 completed rides per month in Bengaluru within 12 months.
